# Privacy Policy — XU-AI Publisher

**Last updated:** 14 November 2026

## Who we are

XU-AI Publisher is a non-commercial tool operated by an individual
developer ("I", "me"). It is used solely to publish videos to my own TikTok
accounts, @catsintheattic08.

Contact: xu.aivideos@gmail.com

## What this app does

XU-AI Publisher connects to TikTok through TikTok's official APIs so I can upload
and publish videos that I created to my own account. It is not offered to the
public, and no one other than me signs in to it.

## Information collected

**From viewers and the public:** none. The app does not collect, receive, or
store any information about people who view, like, or comment on my videos.

**From my own TikTok account:** when I authorise the app through TikTok
Login, TikTok provides:

- an access token and refresh token that let the app post on my behalf
- basic account information (such as my account ID and display name), used
  only to confirm the correct account is connected

The app requests only the permissions needed to publish videos.

## How information is used

TikTok data is used only to:

- authenticate with TikTok
- upload and publish videos to my own account
- check whether an upload succeeded

It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics on other users, or any
other purpose.

## Storage and security

Tokens and account details are stored locally on a private computer that I
control. They are not stored on any public server or website. Access is
restricted to me.

## Sharing

I do not sell, rent, or share TikTok data with anyone. Videos published
through the app are sent to TikTok and are subject to
[TikTok's Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app remains connected to my account. I can
revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings, and I delete the stored
tokens and account details when the app is disconnected or no longer used.

If you believe the app holds any information about you, contact me at the
address above and I will respond and delete it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any
information from anyone under 18.

## Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the "Last updated" date
above.
